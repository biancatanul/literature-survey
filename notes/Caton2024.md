# Caton2024: Fairness in Machine Learning: A Survey

- **Type:** survey
- **Priority:** secondary
- **Status:** own notes, refined
- **Reference:** see `references.bib`, key `Caton2024`

## Summary

Overview of approaches to increase the fairness of ML. Organizes them into pre-, in- and post-processing, explains where unfairness comes from, and presents the main fairness definitions, metrics and methods.

## Use in my thesis

Background on mitigation categories. Helps place my tool in the pipeline: detection at the data level, before any mitigation.
