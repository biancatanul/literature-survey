# Dwork2012: Fairness through Awareness

- **Type:** conference paper (theory)
- **Priority:** secondary
- **Reference:** see `references.bib`, key `Dwork2012`

## Summary

Introduces individual fairness: similar individuals should be treated similarly, formalized as a Lipschitz condition on the classifier given a task-specific similarity metric. Also discusses how group-level statistical parity can still treat individuals unfairly, and relates the two notions.

## Use in my thesis

Metrics chapter: contrast individual fairness with group fairness. Possible justification for focusing my tool on group-level metrics, since individual fairness needs a task-specific similarity metric that is hard to define for an arbitrary uploaded dataset.
