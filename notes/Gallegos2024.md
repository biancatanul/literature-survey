# Gallegos2024: Bias and Fairness in Large Language Models: A Survey

- **Type:** survey
- **Priority:** secondary (edge)
- **Reference:** see `references.bib`, key `Gallegos2024`

## Summary

Survey of bias and fairness in large language models. Provides taxonomies of metrics and datasets and covers mitigation methods for text generation.

## Use in my thesis

Scope statement: my work targets tabular datasets, so LLM bias is out of scope, and this survey can be cited once to show awareness of that area. The taxonomy of metrics may also be useful as a comparison point.
