# Dodge2019: Explaining Models: An Empirical Study of How Explanations Impact Fairness Judgment

- **Type:** user study
- **Priority:** secondary
- **Reference:** see `references.bib`, key `Dodge2019`

## Summary

Studies how the style of an explanation changes how people judge the fairness of an ML model. A logistic regression predicts recidivism on the COMPAS data, once trained on raw data and once on data processed by a debiasing preprocessor. 160 Mechanical Turk workers rated the fairness of predictions after seeing one of four automatically generated explanations.

## Use in my thesis

Plain-language explanations and a possible model auditing extension.
