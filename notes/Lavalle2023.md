# Lavalle2023: A Data Analytics Methodology to Visually Analyze the Impact of Bias and Rebalancing

- **Type:** journal article
- **Priority:** primary
- **Reference:** see `references.bib`, key `Lavalle2023`

## Summary

Extended version of [Lavalle2020](Lavalle2020.md). Argues that rebalancing a biased dataset is a common remedy but not a neutral transformation, and that it can harm the results of the analysis. Proposes a visualization-based methodology in which users define their analytical requirements, bias is then detected and visualized automatically, and users decide whether rebalancing is appropriate. Contributions: the methodology, a metamodel for visually representing bias, and an evaluation on a real-world case study showing that rebalancing is not always the best option.

## Use in my thesis

Closest existing work (automatic bias detection plus visualization). For my recommendation feature, the lesson is that suggestions should warn about the side effects of rebalancing and never recommend it blindly.
