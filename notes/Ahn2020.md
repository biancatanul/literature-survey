# Ahn2020: FairSight: Visual Analytics for Fairness in Decision Making

- **Type:** visualization tool
- **Priority:** secondary
- **Reference:** see `references.bib`, key `Ahn2020`

## Summary

FairSight is built to achieve different notions of fairness in ranking decisions through understanding, measuring, diagnosing and mitigating bias. It covers individual and group fairness.

## Use in my thesis

Visualization design and the overall workflow (understand, measure, diagnose, mitigate).
