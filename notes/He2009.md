# He2009: Learning from Imbalanced Data

- **Type:** survey
- **Priority:** secondary
- **Reference:** see `references.bib`, key `He2009`

## Summary

Survey of the class imbalance problem, where one class dominates the data and classifiers end up favoring it. Covers the nature of the problem, sampling methods, cost-sensitive learning, and evaluation metrics for imbalanced data.

## Use in my thesis

Define representation imbalance and separate it from outcome disparities and fairness metrics. Also a background for the rebalancing discussion in [Lavalle2023](Lavalle2023.md).
