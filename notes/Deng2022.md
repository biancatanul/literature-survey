# Deng2022: Exploring How Machine Learning Practitioners (Try To) Use Fairness Toolkits

- **Type:** user study
- **Priority:** primary
- **Reference:** see `references.bib`, key `Deng2022`

## Summary

11 practitioners explored AIF360 and Fairlearn and then used one of them to assess and mitigate unfairness on a student-performance dataset. 9 of 11 treated sex as sensitive by default, 7 of those dropped the sensitive features assuming that fixed fairness, and none checked for proxies. Participants valued Fairlearn's scikit-learn-like API. They found the metric names unintuitive and the documentation short on when and why to use each metric. The authors call for support at the data exploration stage and for reports aimed at non-engineers.

## Use in my thesis

Tool comparison, and the argument that non-expert reports are needed.
