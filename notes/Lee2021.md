# Lee2021: The Landscape and Gaps in Open Source Fairness Toolkits

- **Type:** survey and user study
- **Priority:** primary
- **Reference:** see `references.bib`, key `Lee2021`

## Summary

Compares 6 open-source fairness toolkits, with their strengths and weaknesses. The authors also run interviews, a survey and an exploratory focus group to learn what practitioners need. The result is a set of gaps between those needs and the toolkits' capabilities.

## Use in my thesis

Tool comparison, and a source for the gaps and criteria my application should fill.
