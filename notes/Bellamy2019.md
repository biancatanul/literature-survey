# Bellamy2019: AI Fairness 360: An Extensible Toolkit for Detecting and Mitigating Algorithmic Bias

- **Type:** tool paper
- **Priority:** primary
- **Reference:** see `references.bib`, key `Bellamy2019`

## Summary

Describes AI Fairness 360 (AIF360), an extensible open-source toolkit with fairness metrics for datasets and models, explanations of those metrics, and bias mitigation algorithms. Available in Python and R.

## Use in my thesis

Comparison with a similar existing tool, and a reference list of dataset-level metrics (it can compute them without a trained model). AIF360 is also one of the tools recommended in the thesis description.
