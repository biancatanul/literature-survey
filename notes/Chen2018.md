# Chen2018: Why Is My Classifier Discriminatory?

- **Type:** conference paper
- **Priority:** primary
- **Reference:** see `references.bib`, key `Chen2018`

## Summary

Argues that unfairness should be analyzed by splitting differences in model performance into bias, variance and noise. Shows that collecting more data or better features can fix unfairness more effectively than constraining the model.

## Use in my thesis

Supports my recommendation feature and the idea that auditing the data matters.
