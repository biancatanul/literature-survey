# Hardt2016: Equality of Opportunity in Supervised Learning

- **Type:** conference paper (metric)
- **Priority:** primary
- **Reference:** see `references.bib`, key `Hardt2016`

## Summary

Proposes a criterion for discrimination against a sensitive attribute in supervised learning. Equalized odds requires equal error behavior across groups; equal opportunity is a weaker version that constrains only the group with the positive outcome. Shows how to adjust a learned predictor to meet the criterion.
