# Pessach2022: A Review on Fairness in Machine Learning

- **Type:** survey
- **Priority:** primary
- **Reference:** see `references.bib`, key `Pessach2023`

## Summary

Overview of the main concepts of identifying, measuring and improving fairness, with the main focus on classification tasks. Covers fairness definitions and measures, divides fairness-enhancing mechanisms into pre-process, in-process and post-process, compares the mechanisms, and reviews emerging research areas of fairness.

## Use in my thesis

Background and definitions chapter; reference for how fairness metrics are categorized.
