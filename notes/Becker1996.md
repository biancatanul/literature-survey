# Becker1996: Adult (UCI Machine Learning Repository)

- **Type:** dataset
- **Priority:** secondary
- **Reference:** see `references.bib`, key `Becker1996`

## Summary

Census income dataset from the UCI repository, extracted from the 1994 US Census. The task is to predict whether a person earns more than 50K per year. 48,842 rows and 14 attributes. Commonly used with sex, race and age as sensitive attributes.

Link: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/2/adult

## Use in my thesis

Candidate benchmark dataset for my application. I can check its group imbalance myself in an exploratory analysis and report my own numbers.
