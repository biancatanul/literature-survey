# Wall2026: Human Bias in Visual Data Analysis: A Synthesis of Research to Empower Decision Makers

- **Type:** book
- **Priority:** secondary (edge)
- **Reference:** see `references.bib`, key `Wall2026`

## Summary

Synthesizes research on human biases (cognitive, perceptual, implicit) in visual data analysis, and how to define, detect and mitigate them.

## Use in my thesis

Terminology note that 'bias' has several meanings, and support for designing charts that do not mislead users.
