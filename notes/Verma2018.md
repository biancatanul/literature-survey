# Verma2018: Fairness Definitions Explained

- **Type:** survey
- **Priority:** primary
- **Reference:** see `references.bib`, key `Verma2018`

## Summary

Collects prominent fairness definitions for classification, explains the idea behind each and demonstrates all of them on the German Credit dataset. Shows why the same case can look fair under some definitions and unfair under others.

## Use in my thesis

Metrics chapter; source for plain-language explanations of each metric.
