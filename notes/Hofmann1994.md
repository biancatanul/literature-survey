# Hofmann1994: Statlog (German Credit Data) (UCI Machine Learning Repository)

- **Type:** dataset
- **Priority:** secondary
- **Reference:** see `references.bib`, key `Hofmann1994`

## Summary

Credit scoring dataset from the UCI repository: 1000 loan applicants, 20 attributes, and a good or bad credit risk label. Attributes such as age, sex (encoded together with marital status) and foreign worker status are used as sensitive attributes in fairness work.

Link: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/144/statloggermancreditdata

## Use in my thesis

Small dataset for quick tests of my application. It is the example dataset used in [Verma2018](Verma2018.md), so results can be compared with that paper.
