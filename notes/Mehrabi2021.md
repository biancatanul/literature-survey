# Mehrabi2021: A Survey on Bias and Fairness in Machine Learning

- **Type:** survey
- **Priority:** secondary
- **Reference:** see `references.bib`, key `Mehrabi2021`

## Summary

Covers applications that have shown biases, the sources and types of bias that can affect AI systems, and the domains and subdomains of AI that have been examined. Also presents definitions and types of fairness, methods and observations from other researchers, and open challenges and opportunities.
